\chapter{Dependency Graphs}
\label{app:dep-graphs}

We show dependency graphs for \texttt{GetOperand} and \texttt{SetOperand}, two of the top-level functions used in symbolic execution, as well as for their correspondence lemmas, \texttt{GetOperand\_R} and \texttt{R\_SetOperand}.
Arrows indicate that one definition calls another, or that one lemma is used in the proof of another.
These graphs exclude many helper functions and lemmas that are not essential to the high-level structure.

\depgraph[.55]{GetOperand}{Dependency graph of \texttt{GetOperand}, which resolves a source operand to a DAG index by dispatching on operand type (register, memory, immediate).}[Tree-shaped dependency graph rooted at GetOperand, branching into helper functions for register, memory, and immediate operand cases, with leaf nodes for primitive DAG operations.]

\depgraph[.55]{SetOperand}{Dependency graph of \texttt{SetOperand}, which writes a DAG index to a destination operand, handling register aliasing and memory stores.}[Tree-shaped dependency graph rooted at SetOperand, branching into register-aliasing logic and memory-store helpers, terminating in primitive symbolic state updates.]

\depgraph[.6]{GetOperandR}{Dependency graph of \texttt{GetOperand\_R}, the correspondence lemma relating symbolic \texttt{GetOperand} to concrete operand evaluation.}[Dependency graph rooted at the \texttt{GetOperand\_R} correspondence lemma, with branches showing the symbolic and concrete sides being related, each decomposing into per-operand-type sub-lemmas.]

\depgraph[.4]{RSetOperand}{Dependency graph of \texttt{R\_SetOperand}, the correspondence lemma relating symbolic \texttt{SetOperand} to concrete writing of registers or memory addresses.}[Dependency graph rooted at the \texttt{R\_SetOperand} correspondence lemma, branching into the symbolic and concrete sides of SetOperand and decomposing into per-destination-type sub-lemmas for register and memory cases.]
